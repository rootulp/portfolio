About Rootul Patel

I'm Rootul Patel, an engineering manager leading the Protocol team at Celestia. This is my personal website, where I share my professional background, resume, and links to my public profiles. It is a reference for my own experience, rather than an official website for any of my employers.

I lead an 8-person team responsible for reliability, performance, and scale for the Celestia protocol. My work combines engineering leadership with hands-on protocol development. At Celestia, I led the first consensus-breaking network upgrade and established the framework used by subsequent upgrades.

I've spent 10 years working on distributed systems, including consensus protocols in Go, Ethereum infrastructure at AWS, and data platforms at Palantir. At Amazon Managed Blockchain, I expanded Ethereum support to six AWS regions. At Palantir, I built applications for Foundry.

For a detailed account of my work experience, education, and technical skills, read my resume. You can also explore my code on GitHub or visit my LinkedIn profile. Outside work, I enjoy triathlons and non-fiction.
